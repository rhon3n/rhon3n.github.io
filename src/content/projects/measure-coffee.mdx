## Coffee knowledge in context

measure.coffee connects equipment, brew details, recipes, and guidance to help coffee drinkers make their next decision with the relevant context close at hand.

## Product and engineering

As Founding Engineer, Joel works on product direction and the customer-facing product: the web app, design system, account flows, and releases.

## Built from coffee work

The product combines hands-on coffee knowledge with practical software. It begins with the decisions people make while brewing and keeps the supporting system easy to understand.
