## Coffee knowledge in context

measure.coffee connects equipment, brew details, recipes, and guidance so coffee drinkers can make a better next decision without losing the context that got them there.

## Product and engineering

As Founder & Founding Engineer, Joel is responsible for product direction and customer-facing implementation, including the web application, design system, account flows, and release process.

## Built from operating experience

The product joins hands-on coffee knowledge with practical software design. It starts with the decisions people actually make and makes the supporting system easier to understand.
