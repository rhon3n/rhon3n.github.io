## The work

This case study covers an end-to-end WordPress experience rebuild: translating the brief into a usable information architecture, making responsive interface decisions, preparing migration paths, and leaving a maintainable handoff.

## The constraint

A redesign only works when the publishing workflow and migration are as considered as the interface. The implementation treated structure, presentation, and operational ownership as one system.

## Status

Final client-facing outcomes and launch details are intentionally withheld until they are approved for public use.
