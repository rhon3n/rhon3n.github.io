# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring teams are the primary audience. They arrive with limited time and need to understand Joel Rhine's role, judgment, technical range, and practical operating experience quickly enough to decide whether to continue into selected work or a conversation.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Joel as a software engineer and product builder whose practice grew from coffee, operations, integrations, and field work. Success means a hiring lead can identify the through-line from real operational problems to useful software, verify that claim through concrete projects, and find a clear next step without decoding the interface.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, resourceful, and direct. The experience should feel like a working field manual shaped by engineering discipline: opinionated enough to be memorable, orderly enough to reward scanning, and human enough to reflect an operator who builds for the next person.

## Anti-references

- Chaotic brutalism that mistakes disruption for hierarchy.
- Generic SaaS polish built from rounded cards, soft gradients, and interchangeable marketing patterns.
- Terminal cosplay or decorative technical language that is not supported by the content.

## Design Principles

1. **Make the through-line visible.** Connect coffee, operations, engineering, and product work as one coherent practice.
2. **Prove before persuading.** Let concrete project constraints, decisions, and outcomes carry the argument.
3. **Reward the shortlist scan.** Establish role, scope, and strongest work within the first viewport and keep every deeper path legible.
4. **Structure is the expression.** Use hierarchy, rhythm, rules, and proportion as the primary brand language.
5. **Build for the next person.** Navigation, component states, and case-study patterns must remain understandable and maintainable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet essential accessibility requirements: semantic HTML, keyboard-operable interactions, visible focus states, readable contrast, 44px minimum interactive targets, responsive layouts without horizontal overflow, meaningful alternative text, and respect for reduced-motion preferences.
